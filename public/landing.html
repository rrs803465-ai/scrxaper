Honest privacy

What is protected, and what is not

In transit

Connections to Scrxaper use TLS, so traffic between your device and the server is encrypted on the way.

Secret chats (optional)

1:1 text chats can be end-to-end encrypted, so the server cannot read them. They do not have forward secrecy.

Everything else

Everything outside secret chats, including regular DMs, communities, calls metadata and the feed, is readable by the server.

We would rather tell you plainly than overpromise. Secret chats are text only and one-to-one.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Scrxaper?

A social and messaging web app with DMs, friends, communities, voice channels, group calls, and a feed with reels and stories.

Can I install it?

Yes. On supported browsers an Install app button appears on this page, and you can install Scrxaper as a PWA with incoming-call alerts.

Are my messages end-to-end encrypted?

Only if you use a secret chat, which is optional, 1:1 and text only, without forward secrecy. Other content is readable by the server. Traffic is protected by TLS in transit.

What bots are included?

Four premade bots: Security, Music, Greeter and Fun.

What is the AI buddy?

A built-in AI companion you can chat with from inside the app.

How do I customize it?

Open Studio in the app to change how Scrxaper looks.
